UltimateTunes is the Android player for your own UltimateTunes server. Search for any song and it plays at once; from then on it lives in your library on your server, tagged with proper metadata and cover art.

<b>Listen</b>
• Four tabs: Listen Now, Radio, Library and Search, with large artwork and bold titles.
• Full-screen player with colours taken from the cover, the queue of what plays next, and synced lyrics: the current line lights up, tap a line to jump to it.
• Start a radio station from any song.
• ReplayGain for an even volume between songs.

<b>Save mobile data</b>
• Every song you play is kept in a cache on the phone, so the second play costs nothing.
• The next songs of the queue are fetched ahead of time.
• Keep albums, playlists or single songs offline, on Wi-Fi only if you like.
• Export songs you have on the phone to a folder of your choice, with their tags.

<b>Your library</b>
• Playlists, favourites, artists and albums, shared with every device you connect.
• Import playlists from a link or a file.
• Light and dark themes, English and Spanish, large fonts and TalkBack.

<b>Private by design</b>
• The app talks only to your server, with a key you can revoke per device. The key is stored encrypted on the phone.
• No ads, no tracking, no analytics. Free software (GPL-3.0-or-later).

Requires an UltimateTunes server, which you run yourself (Docker). It also works as a client of the server's OpenSubsonic API.
